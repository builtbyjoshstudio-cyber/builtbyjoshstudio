Oiling an End-Grain Board

Board care·5 minutes·September 2026

This end-grain teak board went bone dry — pale, chalky, thirsty. Food-safe board oil brings the whole thing back: both faces, the edges, the handle grooves, buffed to a deep glow. No talking and no rush, just the real sounds of pour, spread, let it drink, buff.

Board care in one line: when the wood looks pale, wash it, dry it fully, flood it with oil, give it a few minutes to drink, then buff off whatever's left. End grain is thirstier than face grain — let it take what it wants.

One-Skillet Spicy Sausage Hash

15-minute cut·August 29, 2026

Sausage, potatoes, carrots, sweet peppers, a jalapeño, onion, and garlic — all in one skillet, and the only thing you have to get right is the order things go in the pan. The sausage browns first so everything after it cooks in that fat. A splash of chicken stock goes in, the lid goes on, and it cooks down until the broth turns into a glaze.

Ground Beef Bulgogi Bowls

15-minute cut·August 25, 2026

Bulgogi without the thin-sliced ribeye and without the restaurant bill. Ground beef, a marinade of serranos, garlic, soy, honey and ginger — and one thing you're not expecting in it: fresh grapefruit juice. The beef browns and comes out so the vegetables fry in its fat, then everything goes back in and the sauce reduces down to a finish of cold butter.

Served over rice measured the way Josh's dad taught him: water to the first knuckle, no cup involved.
