The Book

Real recipes, the way we actually cook them

Everything in here is real — the dishes my family actually makes, including the shortcuts, the store-bought buns, the rotisserie chickens, and the fish sauce that ends up on frozen pizza. The ratios are starting points, doorways, never destinations: the recipe is your first attempt; your tongue does the rest.

What's Inside

Seven chapters, one pattern

Almost everything starts from the same idea — salty, sweet, garlic, heat. Learn the sauces first and the rest of the book opens up.

01

Nước Mắm

The sauces that run the whole book — starting with the fish sauce my dad made at the same counter where he taught me to cook.

02

Summer Rolls (Gỏi Cuốn)

Fresh rolls the way we make them at home — and the rolling technique that stops them falling apart.

03

Bún Bowls

Rice-noodle bowls built to balance — herbs, protein, pickles, and the dressing that ties it together.

04

Over Rice

The weeknight backbone — the dishes my family actually eats over a pot of rice.

05

Fried Spring Rolls (Chả Giò)

The crispy ones — including which shortcuts are worth it and which aren't.

06

Bánh Mì, My Way

Store-bought bread, homemade everything else — a sandwich that doesn't apologize.

07

Phở

The twelve-hour one. Worth every hour, and the book walks you through all of them.
