What Makes the Rabbit, the Rabbit

  • Element: Wood
  • Yin/Yang: Yin
  • Order in Zodiac: 4th
  • Years (most recent): 2023, 2011, 1999, 1987
  • Compatible Trine: Rabbit, Goat, Pig

The Rabbit is the 4th animal in the Chinese zodiac cycle. Associated with the Wood element and Yin energy, the Rabbit represents gentleness, elegance, and quiet perceptiveness in Chinese tradition. Rabbit years run on a 12-year cycle — 2023, 2011, 1999, 1987, and so on. The Rabbit's compatible trine includes the Goat and Pig.

Born in the Year of the Rabbit

The Rabbit crossed the river by hopping stones and, when it ran out of stones, riding a floating log — fourth place through nimbleness rather than strength. The Rabbit is the fourth sign of the cycle, Yin in polarity and tied to the Wood element. Rabbit years are associated with gentleness, good taste and caution: diplomatic people who avoid confrontation, notice details others miss, and build calm, comfortable homes.

Rabbit years and their elements: 1951 (Metal Rabbit), 1963 (Water Rabbit), 1975 (Wood Rabbit), 1987 (Fire Rabbit), 1999 (Earth Rabbit), 2011 (Metal Rabbit), 2023 (Water Rabbit), 2035 (Wood Rabbit). Lunar New Year falls in late January or February, so a January or early-February birthday usually belongs to the previous animal — check the exact date for your year.

Which signs is the Rabbit compatible with?

The Rabbit shares a trine with the Goat and Pig — the traditional best matches for friendship, partnership and business. Its opposite on the wheel is the Rooster, the pairing classical astrology treats as the most challenging (and the most interesting).
