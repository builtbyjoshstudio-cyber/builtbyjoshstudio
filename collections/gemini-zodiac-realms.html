What is a Zodiac Realm?

A Zodiac Realm is a hybrid cosmos-landscape rendering of a zodiac sign — not a portrait of the sign, but the mythic environment the sign would inhabit. Each Western sign has 4 unique realm titles, with 2 variants each, totaling 8 designs per sign.

What's included in the Gemini bundle?

All 8 Gemini realm designs across 4 realm titles (Reflections Between Worlds, The Mirrored Divide, The Mirrored Spiral, The Skybound Library), each with 2 variants. Files come in 3 sizes (4800×4800, 4800×6000, 6000×9000) and 2 formats (JPG + PNG). 48 files in total.
