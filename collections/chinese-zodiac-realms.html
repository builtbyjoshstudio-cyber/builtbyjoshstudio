What's Included With the Bundle

The Chinese Zodiac Realms Bundle is a complete digital download. After purchase, you get instant access to 144 image files organized by animal — no shipping, no waiting, no physical product mailed to you.

Each Realm design is delivered in three aspect ratios: 1:1 square (4800 × 4800 pixels), 4:5 portrait (4800 × 6000 pixels), and 2:3 portrait (6000 × 9000 pixels). Every size is provided in both PNG (lossless) and JPG (92% quality) formats. Both are tailored to the Chinese Realms Collection.
