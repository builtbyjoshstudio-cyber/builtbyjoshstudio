What is a Chinese Zodiac Realm?

A Chinese Zodiac Realm is a hybrid cosmos-landscape rendering of one of the 12 Chinese zodiac animals — not a portrait of the animal itself, but the mythic environment that animal would inhabit. Each animal has its own uniquely named realm with two distinct variants.

What's included in the bundle?

All 24 Chinese zodiac realm designs — one named realm per animal (e.g., the Dragon's Radiant Dominion, the Dog's Vigilant Light) with 2 variants each. Files come in 3 sizes (4800×4800, 4800×6000, 6000×9000) and 2 formats (JPG + PNG).
