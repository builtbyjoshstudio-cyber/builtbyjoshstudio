What Makes Aries, Aries

  • Element: Fire
  • Modality: Cardinal
  • Ruling Planet: Mars
  • Symbol: The Ram
  • Dates: March 21 – April 19
  • Sister Fire Signs: Leo, Sagittarius

Aries is the first sign of the zodiac, spanning March 21 – April 19. It is a fire sign ruled by Mars — the planet of drive, aggression, and action. Aries is represented by the ram, a symbol of headfirst momentum and refusal to back down.

People born under Aries are often described as bold, competitive, and direct. They tend to be initiators — the ones who start the project, make the first move, and set the pace. Aries energy is forward motion. It doesn't wait for permission and it doesn't look back.

That energy defines this entire collection. Whether rendered in the gold-line sacred geometry of the Celestial Animals, the molten authority of the Mythic Guardians, or the crimson menace of the Halloween Horrors — every Aries piece is designed to feel like it's moving, even standing still.
