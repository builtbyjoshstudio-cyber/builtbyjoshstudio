What Makes the Snake, the Snake

  • Element: Fire
  • Yin/Yang: Yin
  • Order in Zodiac: 6th
  • Years (most recent): 2025, 2013, 2001, 1989
  • Compatible Trine: Ox, Snake, Rooster

The Snake is the 6th animal in the Chinese zodiac cycle. Associated with the Fire element and Yin energy, the Snake represents wisdom, intuition, and quiet intelligence in Chinese tradition. Snake years run on a 12-year cycle — 2025, 2013, 2001, 1989, and so on. The Snake's compatible trine includes the Ox and Rooster.

Born in the Year of the Snake

The Snake crossed hidden in the Horse's hoof and startled it at the last moment, slipping into sixth place — the legend's lesson in quiet strategy. The Snake is the sixth sign of the cycle, Yin in polarity and tied to the Fire element. Snake people are described as wise, private and intuitive: graceful, observant, slow to trust and very hard to fool. They prefer depth to noise and finish what matters.

Snake years and their elements: 1953 (Water Snake), 1965 (Wood Snake), 1977 (Fire Snake), 1989 (Earth Snake), 2001 (Metal Snake), 2013 (Water Snake), 2025 (Wood Snake), 2037 (Fire Snake). Lunar New Year falls in late January or February, so a January or early-February birthday usually belongs to the previous animal — check the exact date for your year.

Which signs is the Snake compatible with?

The Snake shares a trine with the Ox and Rooster — the traditional best matches for friendship, partnership and business. Its opposite on the wheel is the Pig, the pairing classical astrology treats as the most challenging (and the most interesting).

A refined, deliberate gift for a 1989, 2001 or 2013 birthday, a 2025 baby's room, or an office wall that wants something elegant and a little mysterious. Every file is print-ready, and the license covers personal use and small print-on-demand runs.
