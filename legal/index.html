What's the Same Across Every License

Whichever Collection you buy from, every BBJ Studio license includes the same core terms. The differences between the five license PDFs are limited to bundle-specific language — which Collection the license covers and what files are included. The legal substance below applies universally.

What you can do with the digital files

  • View, display, and print them for your own personal use
  • Produce physical print-on-demand products (framed prints, posters, canvases, mugs, t-shirts, phone cases, and similar tangible goods) up to a cumulative cap of 100 physical units per individual design, across all formats, vendors, channels, and time periods combined
  • Use the prints as personal or commercial gifts within the same 100-print-per-design cap

What you cannot do

  • Redistribute, share, sell, or transmit the digital files themselves to anyone, in any format, by any means
  • Use the files as input, training data, reference data, or seed images for any AI model, machine learning system, or generative model
  • Mint or tokenize the files as NFTs or other blockchain assets
  • Exceed the 100-print-per-design cap (it does not reset and is cumulative across all channels)
  • Claim authorship of the designs, register them as your own intellectual property, or remove copyright notices and metadata
  • Modify the designs in a way that misrepresents them as your own original work

AI disclosure

Every BBJ Studio design is created using a combination of human creative direction and AI image generation tools, including Leonardo.ai. Selection, curation, refinement, post-processing, and final arrangement are all done by the studio's founder. This is disclosed in full in every License Agreement.

Governing law

All licenses are governed by Kansas law, with disputes resolved exclusively in Kansas state or federal courts. Built by Josh Studio LLC is a Kansas limited liability company (Kansas Business ID 10076138).
