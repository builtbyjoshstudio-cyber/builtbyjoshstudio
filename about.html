About the Studio

One person. Three brands. Zero filler.

Built by Josh Studio is a one-person creative studio in Kansas, building digital products and art that I'd actually use myself. If it doesn't hold up in my own workflow, it doesn't leave the studio.

Hi, I'm Josh.

Built by Josh Studio started in 2025 as a one-person creative practice and became Built by Josh Studio LLC in 2026 — a small set of well-designed digital products under one roof. The work splits across three distinct lines, by design — productivity tools that solve a real problem, art that means something on a wall, and children's educational books for kids. Each line is its own brand, with its own voice and visual identity, but everything ships from the same studio with the same standard: real-world tested, no bloat, no filler.

I run all of it solo. That's not a constraint — it's the point. Every workbook formula, every Notion module, every zodiac palette is decided by one person who actually uses the thing. There's no committee, no roadmap-by-vote, no "minimum viable product" with broken corners. The studio is small on purpose so the work can be opinionated and complete.

What I bring to the work

I've been cooking daily for about 25 years — I learned by watching my father most nights growing up, and I still use the finger rice method he taught me.

I'm a published novelist writing two series. Overlayed Echoes — a near-future LitRPG about friends, games, and what happens when the story turns on the storyteller — is available on Amazon today as the full-length first book of a five-book arc. The second series, Ebonspire Chronicles, is a dark fantasy noir following an immortal detective in a volcano-built city of stratified magic and institutional rot.

Built by Josh Studio LLC is the Kansas company I formed to run both brands, and every Tynkr Tools & Co product — every budget workbook, every Notion OS template — has been in my own budget or my own workflow for months before it shipped. Design, build, copy, support, ops — all of it is one person across both brands, and that's the only way the work stays this opinionated.

Where the Studio Lives

Built by Josh Studio is based in Kansas. All products are digital and delivered as instant downloads. Customer support, pre-purchase questions, and partnership inquiries all come to one inbox: josh@builtbyjoshstudio.com. Response time is typically 1–2 business days.

Tynkr Tools & Co products are sold on Etsy and on this site directly. Zodiac art prints are on the BBJ Studio Etsy shop. Both shops handle digital delivery automatically — no waiting, no accounts.
