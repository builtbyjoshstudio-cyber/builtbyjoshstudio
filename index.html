Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked

What's the difference between Tynkr Tools & Co and Built by Josh Studio?

Built by Josh Studio is the parent studio. Tynkr Tools & Co is the digital productivity brand — Notion OS templates and Excel/Google Sheets workbooks. Built by Josh Studio also publishes zodiac digital art prints. Same studio, two distinct product lines.

Where do I buy the templates and spreadsheets?

You buy direct on this site — check out through the secure overlay and download instantly, with no account required. Etsy is available as a secondary option for the Tynkr templates, workbooks, and cookbook; the zodiac art bundles are sold here (the Built by Josh Studio Etsy shop carries individual prints and other studio work).

Do the Notion templates work with the free version of Notion?

Yes. Every Notion OS template works on both the free and paid versions of Notion. You don't need a subscription to use them.

Can I use the budget spreadsheets in both Excel and Google Sheets?

Most spreadsheet products are available as an Excel version, a Google Sheets version, or a bundle that includes both. Check the individual product page for formats.

Do I get updates after I buy?

The Notion and Google Sheets versions update automatically — you work from the live linked copy, so you always have the latest version at no extra cost. Excel files are a one-time download you keep.

Who is Josh?

Josh is a solo creator based in Kansas. He builds every Notion template, spreadsheet, and zodiac print in the studio, and is the author of Overlayed Echoes — his debut near-future LitRPG novel — and the dark fantasy detective noir series Ebonspire Chronicles.
