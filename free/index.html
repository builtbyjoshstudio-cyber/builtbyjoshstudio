Frequently asked questions about Free Lite versions and tools

Why do the Lite templates use a $0 checkout while browser tools require no signup?

Lite templates and workbooks use a $0 email checkout so Lemon Squeezy can deliver your Notion duplicate link or Excel and Google Sheets files immediately. The free browser tools run locally in your browser with no signup or account.

How do the Lite versions differ from the full paid templates?

Each Lite edition gives you a working core slice of the full Notion system or multi-tab workbook so you can test the workflow on real data. Full paid editions unlock all connected databases, dashboards, and complete multi-tab automation.

Does downloading a free Lite edition include an upgrade discount?

Yes. Every Lite download includes a 35% upgrade discount inside the delivered template or workbook files, applicable toward the corresponding full paid version.

Do the free browser tools track or upload what I type?

No. Every kitchen, money, and tabletop utility across the Built by Josh Studio subdomains runs client-side in your browser, is completely ad-free, and never requires an account.
