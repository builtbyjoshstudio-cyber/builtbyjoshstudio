The Fiction

I also write fiction. As J.S. Warden.

I write the kind of books I'd want to disappear into for a weekend. Two series so far — one a sci-fi story about friendship and a game that turns real, the other a dark fantasy noir about a detective who can't stay clean.

J.S. Warden is the pen name of Josh, the creator behind Built By Josh Studio.

Overlayed Echoes

The first book of a five-book series.

When mandatory neural chips make tabletop roleplaying real, a group of lifelong friends gets exactly what they wished for — until something goes wrong.

It's a story about friendship and what happens when the games you love stop being games.

Ebonspire Chronicles

A dark fantasy detective noir series.

Ebonspire is a vertical city carved into a dead volcano — and someone is freezing its citizens alive. The detective on the case is brilliant, immortal, and quietly falling apart. A dark fantasy noir about grief, addiction, and the cost of the truth.

Book One is in final formatting and releasing soon. It's a separate world from Overlayed Echoes — no connection between the two.

Questions

Who is J.S. Warden?

J.S. Warden is the pen name of Josh, the creator behind Built By Josh Studio. He writes the science-fiction series Overlayed Echoes and the dark-fantasy noir series Ebonspire Chronicles.

How many Overlayed Echoes books are there?

Overlayed Echoes is Book One of a planned five-book series. The first book is available now on Amazon.

Is Ebonspire Chronicles connected to Overlayed Echoes?

No — they're separate series with no shared world, characters, or tone. Overlayed Echoes is near-future science fiction; Ebonspire Chronicles is dark fantasy detective noir.
