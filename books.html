The Fiction

Fiction by J.S. Warden — near-future LitRPG & dark fantasy noir

I write the kind of books I'd want to disappear into for a weekend. Two series so far: Overlayed Echoes, a near-future LitRPG about friendship and a game that turns real — and Ebonspire Chronicles, a dark fantasy detective noir about an immortal detective who can't stay clean.

J.S. Warden is the pen name of Josh, the creator behind Built By Josh Studio.

Overlayed Echoes

Near-future LitRPG · the first book of a five-book series.

When mandatory neural chips make tabletop roleplaying real, a group of lifelong friends gets exactly what they wished for — until something goes wrong.

It's a story about friendship and what happens when the games you love stop being games.

For readers who love LitRPG and GameLit, immersive game worlds, and stories about friendship, found family, and what you'd risk to stay in the game.

Free on Kindle Unlimited · also in paperback & Kindle.

Ebonspire Chronicles

A dark fantasy detective noir series.

Ebonspire is a vertical city carved into a dead volcano — and someone is freezing its citizens alive. The detective on the case is brilliant, immortal, and quietly falling apart. A dark fantasy noir about grief, addiction, and the cost of the truth.

For readers who love dark fantasy, hard-boiled detective noir, morally gray immortals, and slow-burn mysteries about grief, addiction, and the cost of the truth.

Book One releases June 2026. It's a separate world from Overlayed Echoes — no connection between the two.

Questions

What genre is Overlayed Echoes?

Overlayed Echoes is a near-future LitRPG (also called GameLit). Set in 2045, it follows a group of lifelong friends who use mandatory neural chips to make tabletop roleplaying physically real — until something goes wrong. It is the first book of a planned five-book series.

What genre is Ebonspire Chronicles?

Ebonspire Chronicles is a dark fantasy detective noir. It follows an immortal, addicted detective investigating a string of murders in Ebonspire — a vertical city carved into a dead volcano. It is a story about grief, addiction, and the cost of the truth.

Are the two series connected?

No. Overlayed Echoes and Ebonspire Chronicles are completely separate worlds with no shared characters or storylines. You can start with either one.

Where can I read these books?

Overlayed Echoes is available now on Amazon in Kindle and paperback, and free to read on Kindle Unlimited. Ebonspire Chronicles releases June 2026 and will also be on Amazon in Kindle and paperback, free on Kindle Unlimited.

Is there an audiobook?

An audiobook of the original edition of Overlayed Echoes is available on Amazon. Note that the current edition is an expanded, full-length novel, so the audiobook covers the shorter original version. An expanded-edition audiobook has not been decided yet.

When does the next book come out?

Ebonspire Chronicles releases June 2026. Book two of Overlayed Echoes is in the works, with writing planned to begin later in 2026 once both current books are out.
