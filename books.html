The Fiction

Fiction by J.S. Warden — near-future LitRPG & dark fantasy noir

J.S. Warden is the pen name of Josh, the creator behind Built By Josh Studio.

Questions

What genre is Overlayed Echoes?

Overlayed Echoes is a dark LitRPG — progression fantasy with survival-horror stakes, mechanically a near-future LitRPG (also called GameLit). Set in 2045, it follows a group of lifelong friends who use mandatory neural chips to make tabletop roleplaying physically real — until something goes wrong. It is the first book of a planned five-book series.

What genre is Ebonspire Chronicles?

Ebonspire Chronicles is a dark fantasy detective noir. It follows an immortal, addicted detective investigating a string of murders in Ebonspire — a vertical city carved into a dead volcano. It is a story about grief, addiction, and the cost of the truth.

Are the two series connected?

No. Overlayed Echoes and Ebonspire Chronicles are completely separate worlds with no shared characters or storylines. You can start with either one.

Where can I read these books?

Overlayed Echoes is available now on Amazon in Kindle and paperback, and free to read on Kindle Unlimited. Ebonspire Chronicles is out now on Amazon in Kindle, paperback, and hardcover — free to read on Kindle Unlimited.

Is there an audiobook?

An audiobook of the original edition of Overlayed Echoes is available on Amazon. Note that the current edition is an expanded, full-length novel, so the audiobook covers the shorter original version. An expanded-edition audiobook has not been decided yet.

When does the next book come out?

Ebonspire Chronicles Book One is out now. Book two of Overlayed Echoes is in the works, with writing planned to begin later in 2026.
