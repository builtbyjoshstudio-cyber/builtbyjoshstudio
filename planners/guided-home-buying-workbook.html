What's Inside

A guided six-part journey

Home buying isn't one decision — it's a hundred, in order. The workbook walks the real path, from the first affordability check to the first year in the house.

01

What Can You Afford?

Affordability worksheets, debt-to-income, and a true monthly-cost picture beyond the mortgage payment.

02

The Down Payment

Savings targets, closing-cost estimates, and the trade-offs between putting more down and keeping reserves.

03

Shop the Mortgage

Side-by-side lender and loan comparisons so you can see the real cost difference, not just the rate.

04

Find the Home

Home-tour scorecards to compare every showing on the same terms — and remember which house was which.

05

Offer & Close

Offer-strategy notes, an inspection checklist, and a closing-day walkthrough so nothing gets missed.

06

The First Year

New-owner costs, a maintenance calendar, and a first-year budget for life after the keys.
