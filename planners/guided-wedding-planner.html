What's Inside

A guided six-part journey

No blank pages staring back at you. The planner moves in the order a real wedding does — set the budget first, then build outward to the day itself and beyond.

01

Set Your Budget

A master budget, contribution tracker, and category breakdowns so the numbers are settled before anything gets booked.

02

Build Your Guest List

Guest list, RSVP & headcount tracker, and meal counts — the headcount that drives every other decision.

03

Book Your Vendors

Side-by-side vendor comparison, contract tracker, and a payment schedule so no deposit slips through.

04

Plan the Details

Seating charts, menu & bar, music & playlist, attire & fittings, registry, and the DIY tracker.

05

The Big Day

Ceremony plan, a minute-by-minute day-of timeline, the photo shot list, and the rehearsal dinner.

06

After the Wedding

Gifts & thank-you tracker, a day-after brunch planner, and the honeymoon itinerary & packing list.
