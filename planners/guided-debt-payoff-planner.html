What's Inside

Six parts, one direction: zero

Not a lecture about debt. The planner gets the whole picture on paper first, helps you pick the method you'll actually stick with, then hands you the trackers that make progress visible.

01

Face Your Debt

A full debt inventory across a two-page spread, a total-debt snapshot, and a worksheet for tackling it as a couple — the whole picture, on paper, once.

02

Choose Your Method

The snowball and the avalanche, each explained plainly, a "which one fits you" worksheet, your payoff order, and your projected debt-free date.

03

Free Up the Money

Find the extra dollars that speed everything up — trimming expenses, negotiating bills, and pointing found money at the plan.

04

Attack & Track

The heart of the book: a color-in debt-free thermometer, a balance-over-time chart, 12 per-debt trackers, 24 monthly check-ins, and payment logs.

05

Protect Your Progress

How credit scores work, a score tracker, and the no-new-debt habits — so the balances you kill stay dead.

06

Freedom

Milestone pages, what to do with the freed-up payment, and a debt-free certificate to sign when you get there.
