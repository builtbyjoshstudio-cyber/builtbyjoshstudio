What's Inside

Six parts, one direction: up

Not a lecture about compound interest. The planner gets your starting number on paper, builds the deposit habit, then hands you challenge after challenge that makes saving feel like a game you're winning.

01

Face Your Number

A starting snapshot, a savings-rate worksheet, and where to keep the money — so you know exactly where you stand before the first deposit.

02

Build the Habit

Pay-yourself-first, automation, and the deposit rhythm that makes saving happen without willpower doing the heavy lifting.

03

The Emergency Fund

A step-by-step plan with two color-in thermometers — your first $1,000, then the full cushion — plus a deposit log, emergency rules, and a refill plan.

04

The Challenges

The heart of the book: the 52-week challenge, the 100-envelope color-in grid, a no-spend month, the $5-bill challenge, round-ups, a pantry challenge, and build-your-own pages.

05

Sinking Funds

Save ahead for the expenses you can see coming — a plain-English explainer plus 10 named fund trackers.

06

Keep It

Milestone markers, what to do when a fund is full, and a Fully Funded certificate to sign at the finish line.
