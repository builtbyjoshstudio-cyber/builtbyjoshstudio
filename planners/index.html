Frequently asked questions about The Guided Series

Are the Guided Series planners dated or undated?

Every planner in The Guided Series is undated, so you can start in any month and move at your own pace without wasted pages.

What size and format are the Guided Series print planners?

All five workbooks are full-size 8.5 × 11 inch paperbacks printed on demand by Amazon, ranging from 97 to 150 pages. Printable PDF editions are also available on Etsy for $7.99.

How are the Guided Series paperbacks priced?

The Guided Budget Planner (150 pages) is listed at $14.99 in paperback on Amazon. The Guided Debt Payoff Planner, Home Buying Workbook, Savings Planner, and Wedding Planner are listed at $12.99 in paperback.

Do the print workbooks pair with Tynkr digital spreadsheets?

Yes. The Guided Budget Planner is the print companion to the Ultimate Budget Workbook, and The Guided Home Buying Workbook is the print companion to the Home Buying & Mortgage Workbook.
