Frequently asked questions about Tynkr templates and workbooks

Do I need a paid Notion account for the Creator OS templates?

No. All five Creator OS Notion templates work on a free Notion plan. Duplicate a template into your workspace and use it right away with no subscription required.

Do the Excel and Google Sheets workbooks include both formats?

Yes. Both the Ultimate Budget Workbook ($22.99) and the Home Buying & Mortgage Workbook ($22.99) ship as one bundle containing both the Excel file and the Google Sheets version.

How much does the Creator OS Full Stack save compared to buying individually?

The five Creator OS templates cost $19.99 each ($99.95 total if bought separately). The Creator OS Full Stack bundle includes all five standalone workspaces for $68.99, saving $30.96.

Can I try a free version before buying?

Yes. Every Tynkr Notion template and spreadsheet workbook has a $0 Lite edition on the Free Lite Versions page. Each Lite download includes a 35% upgrade discount inside its files.
