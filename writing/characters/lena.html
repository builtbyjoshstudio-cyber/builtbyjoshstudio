Overlayed Echoes · Characters

Lena

The Mage · Kael's sister, legally and in spirit

Lena is the mage of the five friends in Overlayed Echoes, and Kael's sister — legally and in spirit. She came up small and quiet, with a stutter born from a hard childhood, before Kael's family adopted her and made her part of the group for good.

The quiet never fully left, but what grew alongside it did: in the game she's all explosive magic — flame, lightning, chaos — the loudest force on the table from the person least likely to raise her voice. She watches more than she speaks, and she reads her brother better than anyone. When the session begins, Lena is watching Kael, already knowing he's carrying something.

Relationships
  • Kael — her adopted brother; she reads him better than anyone.
  • Theo, Angela, Marcus — the rest of the five.

Stage One · The Calm Before — Lena in the hour before the first session, 2045. Her story continues as the world of Overlayed Echoes unfolds.

Stage Two · In the Game

In the game, Lena plays Elyra — a slight mage with storm-gray eyes and a cloak sparking with energy, her magic wild: fire, lightning, ice, tied to a buried past.
