Overlayed Echoes · Characters

Kael

The Game Master · "The one who holds the story together"

Kael is the game master of Overlayed Echoes and the friend who built the game the others step into — eleven months of his life poured into a campaign he drew the maps for himself. The story's narrator, he's the second-quietest person in any room, the kind who carries a worn black notebook everywhere because writing his life down is the only way he's ever figured out how to be inside it.

He loves the four people at his table the way he can't quite say out loud, so he builds it instead — little gifts stitched into the code where he thinks they'll pass unseen. As the first session begins, he reaches for the interface with the countdown caught in his throat.

Relationships
  • Lena — his adopted sister.
  • Angela — his best friend since childhood.
  • Theo — who carried him home once.
  • Marcus — the rest of the five.

Stage One · The Calm Before — Kael in the hour before the first session, 2045. His story continues as the world of Overlayed Echoes unfolds.

Stage Two · In the Game

In the game, Kael steps in as the Wanderer — a hooded NPC, face half-hidden, gripping a rune-etched staff cracked and humming with faint power. Low, cryptic, unreadable. Friend or foe? Only the Game Master knows.
