The Five Friends of Overlayed Echoes

Five friends spent fifteen years rolling dice around the same tables. Then they built a game that made the table real — and stepped inside. Meet them as they were in the hour before the first session: the game master, the guardian, the healer, the trickster, and the mage.

Kael
The Game Master

The quiet one who built the game — and holds the story together.
Theo
The Guardian

The protector who counts the exits before anyone steps inside.
Angela
The Healer

The nurse who settles a room — kind but sharp; Theo's sister.
Marcus
The Trickster

Chaos personified; the laugh that keeps everyone from getting scared.
Lena
The Mage

Quiet and watchful, all explosive magic — Kael's sister.
