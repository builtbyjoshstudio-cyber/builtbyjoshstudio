Overlayed Echoes · Characters

Angela

The Healer · "Kind but sharp"

Angela is the healer of the five friends in Overlayed Echoes, and Theo's sister. She's "kind but sharp — like a friend who'd bring you soup then roast you for catching a cold." A nurse by trade, she's the kind who can hold a hand and run an IV at the same time, and the kind who'll tell a doctor he's wrong in front of the patient when he's wrong in front of the patient.

She talks almost without stopping — about everything except the hard nights she carries home from work. She has settled rooms just by walking into them since she was a kid. When the session begins, Angela is already squared away, steady at the center of the group.

Relationships
  • Theo — her brother, the guardian.
  • Kael — her best friend since childhood; her family treated him as another son.
  • Marcus & Lena — the rest of the five.

Stage One · The Calm Before — Angela in the hour before the first session, 2045. Her story continues as the world of Overlayed Echoes unfolds.
